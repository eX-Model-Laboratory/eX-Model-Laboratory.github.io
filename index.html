Welcome to X-Model Laboratory!

We are eXtreme Model Laboratory, or XML for short, an aerospace and aeronautics organization/club at the Univerity of Texas at Austin.

Our areas of interest:

  • Model rockets
  • Model planes
  • Model hot air baloons
  • Drone flight simulations
  • Space pictures processing
  • Programming
  • Mini projects throughout the year
  • And more!

Meetings, Membership Info, and Schedule

We hold weekly meetings from 7 to 8pm every Friday! (Come early). Room details TBA, so for now we announce it every week in our discord.

No membership fee required! Just show up to our meetings regularly and you will be considered an active member of our club.
