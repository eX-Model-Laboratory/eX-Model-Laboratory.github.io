Other Things

Mini meeting log/about page

Each meeting we have a designated topic that we talk about. So far, we have built small rockets, discussed different types of engines for models, what software to use for modeling, how to process space pictures (like the one you saw on our home page background), model rocket computers and how to make one, etc.

We are preparing to host small programming workshops and challenges, so worry not regarding your coding skills!

We are a club open to all majors. There is no application process nor membership fee, you can just show up to the meetings regularly and do things with us, and then you'll be considered an active member. (๑❛ᴗ❛๑)

We announce most of the stuff through our discord server, so make sure to join that!
